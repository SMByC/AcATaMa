Notes:

  • Accuracy estimated from the sample counts (Stehman and Foody, 2009, Table 21.3).
  • Area proportion of each reference class estimated as n+j/n (Stehman, 2013, Eq. 18), with the variance estimator p(1 - p)/(n - 1), unbiased for simple random sampling with replacement (Cochran, 1977).
  • The variances are the ones of sampling with replacement (without finite population correction), as the random sampling of AcATaMa, whose samples can fall in the same pixel; for samples without replacement, omitting the correction makes them larger, negligibly when the sampling fractions are small, also within each stratum. The variances of the producer's accuracy and of the post-stratified area are large sample approximations.
  • Confidence limits: area ± z · SE. Coefficient of variation: 100 · SE/area. Uncertainty: 100 · z · SE/area (half-width of the confidence interval relative to the area).
  • For systematic samples the standard errors are approximations that usually overestimate the variance (Olofsson et al., 2014).
