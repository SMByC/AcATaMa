# About Us

AcATaMa is developed and maintained by the Forest and Carbon Monitoring System (SMByC) at the
Institute of Hydrology, Meteorology and Environmental Studies (IDEAM) in Colombia. SMByC is
responsible for measuring and ensuring the accuracy of the official national forest figures in
Colombia, as well as activity data related to forest cover change for Greenhouse Gas (GHG)
emissions reporting in the context of the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change
(UNFCCC).

As part of the SMByC, we identified the need to not only establish a systematic and rigorous
approach, but also to streamline and optimize the accuracy assessment procedure
{cite}`Galindo2014`. To address this gap, we developed AcATaMa, an open-source QGIS plugin
specifically designed for the accuracy assessment of thematic raster maps in alignment with
international guidance and good practice, including the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate
Change (IPCC) principles of **transparency, completeness, consistency, comparability, and
accuracy**.

AcATaMa is an official component of our Digital Image Processing Protocol for Quantifying
Deforestation in Colombia {cite}`Galindo2014`, where it plays a central role in our accuracy
assessment workflows. We use AcATaMa to conduct systematic evaluations of thematic maps,
ensuring that our forest monitoring products meet the accuracy standards required for national
reporting.
